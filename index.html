01 / A WORLD WE SHARE SCROLL TO WANDER ↓
THE QUESTION BEHIND THE PROJECT

Wildlife doesn’t follow
the lines we draw.

We divide the world into cities and wilderness. But animals move through landscapes that rarely fit those neat boundaries.

No Longer Wild? is a student research project exploring wildlife in human-populated places: what brings animals there, how people respond, and what it means to share a landscape.

The question in the title is an invitation to look closer. An animal doesn’t stop being wild when it crosses into our world.

02 / THE REASONS

Why here?

Investigate habitat, food, climate, and land use. Different places can tell very different stories.

03 / THE RESPONSE

How do we coexist?

Consider the needs of animals and people, and the trade-offs behind decisions about shared spaces.
